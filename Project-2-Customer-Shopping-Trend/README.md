Project 2: Customer Shopping Analysis

1. Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Microsoft Power BI. The analysis explores customer spending patterns, demographic differences, and purchase frequency to understand how customers interact with products and identify insights that can support business decisions.

An interactive dashboard was developed to present key performance indicators and shopping trends in a clear, accessible format.

2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall customer spending and average purchase value.
- Compare spending patterns across age groups.
- Examine differences in purchasing behavior by gender.
- Analyze customer purchase frequency.
- Build an interactive dashboard to communicate key findings.
- Provide insights that can support customer-focused marketing and business decisions.

3. Dataset Description

The project uses a customer shopping trends dataset containing approximately 3,900 customer records and 19 columns.

The dataset includes information such as customer ID, age, gender, item type, purchase amount, location, purchase frequency, and other shopping-related attributes.

These fields were used to examine customer demographics, spending patterns, and purchasing behavior.

4. Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI: Dashboard development and data visualization.
- Power Query: Data import, inspection, and preparation.
- DAX: Calculations and measures for key performance indicators.

5. Data Preparation and Cleaning

The dataset was imported into Power BI and inspected using Power Query.

Data profiling was used to examine column quality, data types, distinct values, and the completeness of the dataset. An age-group field was created to support demographic comparisons, and a measure was used to analyze the customer count.

The prepared data was then used to develop the required dashboard visuals.

6. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights the following results:

KPI| Result
Total Purchase Amount| $233,081
Average Purchase Amount| $59.76
Customer Count| Approximately 3,900

These KPIs provide an overview of customer spending and the number of distinct customers represented in the analysis.

7. Dashboard Features

The dashboard presents customer shopping insights through the following analyses:

- Overall customer spending.
- Average purchase amount.
- Customer count.
- Spending by age group.
- Spending by gender.
- Purchase frequency analysis.

Together, these visuals help users explore the distribution of spending and differences in shopping behavior across customer segments.

8. Key Findings

8.1 Spending by Age Group

The age group of 25–34 recorded the highest spending at $45,400, followed closely by the 45–54 age group at $45,371. The 55–64 age group recorded $44,096.

These results show that spending was relatively close across the leading age groups in the dataset.

8.2 Spending by Gender

The spending comparison showed:

- Male customers: $157,890 (67.74%).
- Female customers: $75,191 (32.26%).

Male customers accounted for the larger share of total spending in this dataset.

8.3 Purchase Frequency

The purchase-frequency analysis showed the following record counts:

- Quarterly: 1,147.
- Biweekly: 1,089.
- Annually: 572.
- Monthly: 555.
- Weekly: 539.

Quarterly was the most frequently recorded purchase interval, followed by biweekly.

9. Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

1. Understand customer segments: Investigate the needs and preferences of different age groups to support more relevant marketing activities.
2. Review spending patterns: Explore the factors contributing to the higher recorded spending among male customers.
3. Consider purchase frequency: Use purchasing intervals to inform customer engagement and retention strategies.
4. Personalize marketing efforts: Develop campaigns that reflect customer interests and observed shopping behavior.
5. Continue monitoring performance: Regularly analyze spending and purchase patterns to identify changes over time.

These recommendations should be evaluated alongside additional information, such as product preferences, customer needs, and business objectives.

10. Conclusion

The Customer Shopping Analysis project demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform shopping data into meaningful insights through data preparation, calculations, and interactive visualizations.

The analysis highlights differences in spending across age groups and genders, as well as the distribution of purchase frequency. These insights provide a foundation for understanding customer behavior and supporting data-informed business decisions.
